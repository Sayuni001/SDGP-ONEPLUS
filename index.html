OUR PURPOSE

Why One+ School?

Many rural schools in Sri Lanka face difficulties accessing essential educational resources. One+ School aims to connect these schools with people who want to help.

01

The Problem

Rural schools may face shortages of books, classroom furniture, learning equipment and other important educational resources.

02

Our Solution

One+ School proposes a website where verified schools publish their needs and donors offer requested physical items with delivery tracking.

WHAT WE OFFER

Our Key Features

A simple and transparent way to connect rural schools with supporters.

✓

Verified Schools

School profiles are reviewed by authorised administrators to support trustworthy donation requests.

☷

Resource Wishlists

Schools can publish specific educational resource needs, including item quantities.

▣

Physical Donations

Donors can offer requested physical items without online money transfers.

↗

Delivery Tracking

Supporters can follow item-offer progress and view delivery confirmation.
